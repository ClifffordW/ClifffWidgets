Before you start

Starving For Drops is an independent, fan-made tracker and stream overlay generator. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or an official website of Klei Entertainment.

All Don't Starve Together skins, artwork, items, logos, promotional feeds and ambassador rosters belong to Klei Entertainment. Huge thanks to Klei for fostering a welcoming community!

Before you add codes or links

Anything you type into the giveaway card, like a code, a Steam key or a prize link, is packed into the card link you copy. Anyone who has that link can open the card and get the prize.

The code is only lightly scrambled in the link. That keeps it from being read at a glance, but it is not secure. Nothing is sent to or stored on a server. It all stays in your browser and in the link you share.

Viewers always copy and paste codes themselves. The card never fills a code into Klei, Steam or any other site, and your code is never put into the redeem links.

Only hand out codes and links you're allowed to give away, and send each prize to the person it's meant for. Never put passwords, account details or anything private in these boxes. This site isn't run by Klei or Steam and can't check that a code or link works.

How Drops Work

Welcome! Accept the short note first, then have a quick read of how drops work.

Step 1

Link your accounts

Connect your Twitch, Bilibili or Douyu account to your Klei account. Without the link, nothing you earn can reach you.

Twitch Bilibili Douyu
Step 2

Watch a drops stream

Open a live Don't Starve Together stream that has Drops enabled. Watch time adds up over time, so it doesn't have to happen in one sitting, but it counts for one channel at a time.

Step 3

Claim on Twitch

When a reward unlocks, claim it from your Twitch Drops Inventory. Claim each reward as it unlocks so the next one can start counting.

Drops Inventory
Step 4

Open the game

Log in to Don't Starve Together. Claimed items are delivered to your Klei account and show up in your Item Collection.

Good to know

What you can earn

Every drop has up to three rewards, unlocked in order the longer you watch:

  • Profile Flair after 30 minutes
  • Portrait Frame after 90 minutes
  • The item skin after 180 minutes

You can only get each reward once. The current drop is on the Active Drop tab.

Ambassador giveaways

Ambassadors are the streamers Klei works with. Some also run giveaways of their own, like codes or older items. Those are the streamer's own and separate from Twitch drops, so check their stream to see how to enter.

Nothing showing up?

  • Watch on the same Twitch account you linked to Klei.
  • To re-link, press Disconnect in your Klei account, not Remove account.
  • Still stuck? Contact Klei support with your Klei User ID.
